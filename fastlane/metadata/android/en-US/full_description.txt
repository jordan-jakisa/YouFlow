YouFlow is a free YouTube client with a Material 3 Expressive interface. It needs no Google account and no Google services, and it has no ads and no tracking. It is a fork of NewPipe and uses the NewPipe Extractor to read YouTube.

<b>Features</b>
• Home with a "For you" feed that is worked out on your device from your history, likes and subscriptions, with a short note on why each video was picked
• Topic lists on Home: Hacker News picks, Developers, AI builders, Startups, Indie hackers and Hackers, ranked by what several channels in that community point to
• Import your YouTube watch history from Google Takeout to tune For you; it stays on your phone
• Shorts feed with preloading, like, save and share
• Search, channels, playlists, comments, chapters and captions
• Subscriptions without an account, feed groups, import and export (NewPipe backups can be imported)
• Background play, picture in picture, mini player, audio-only mode, sleep timer and a queue
• Downloads
• Local history, watch later and playlists
• Reorderable bottom bar, Shorts can be hidden
• Keeps the screen on while a video plays (can be turned off)

Hacker News picks use the HackerTube dataset, licensed under ODC-By 1.0.

YouFlow is not affiliated with NewPipe or YouTube. Requires Android 12 or newer.
